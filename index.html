Question III

The brain and the LLM share a space

An LLM turns each word into a list of numbers. Multiply that list by one matrix and you get the brain's response to the word.

Timing from Goldstein et al. 2025. Surprise from GPT-2, running in your browser. Brain values are illustrative.

One matrix

brain = W × word

Each row of W is one electrode. Multiply the row by the word's numbers, add it up, and you get that electrode's response.

Fit W once on a few thousand words. It then predicts the brain on words it has never seen.

Four directions

AI → brain

Decoding becomes software. Non-invasive decoding went from 8% to 61% word accuracy.

Brain → AI

Brain data makes models better at meaning. It's the last dataset nobody has scraped.

Model organism

Run the experiment on the model, confirm it in a few people. Faster, cheaper neuroscience.

Brain ↔ brain

Speaker and listener meet in the same space.

Risks

  • Inner speech leaks.
  • Decoders favour the languages they were trained on.
  • LLMs model the language network, not the mind.
